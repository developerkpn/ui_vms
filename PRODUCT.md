# Product: Vendor Management System (VMS)

## Purpose

A web application for KPN to manage vendor registrations, material requests, and approvals.

## Users

Internal employees (Master Data, Managers, Procurement) and external Vendors.

## Tone & Brand

- **Professional & Enterprise**: Reliable, data-driven, and formal.
- **Efficient**: Focus on productivity and clear data entry.
- **Clean & Modern**: Uses Material UI with a professional color palette.

## Strategic Principles

- **Clarity over Creativity**: Form fields and labels must be unambiguous.
- **Consistent Layout**: Follow enterprise patterns for forms and tables.
